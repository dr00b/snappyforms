Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

1. Overview
SnappyForms ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our volunteer and work hour tracking platform ("Platform").

2. Information We Collect
We collect the following types of information to facilitate benefit activity tracking and verification:

Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, password, and account role (Beneficiary or Community Organization).

Beneficiary Identifier Data: Case numbers, state benefit identification numbers, or relevant demographic details required by state program forms (SNAP/Medicare).

Activity & Engagement Logs: Dates, total hours, descriptions of volunteer/work activities, supervisor contact details, and location data where service was completed.

Verification Documents: Supporting files uploaded to verify hours (e.g., signed timesheets, activity logs, or certificates).

3. How We Use Your Information
We use collected data solely to fulfill our non-profit mission of assisting users with benefit compliance:

Generating verified activity reports for submission to state or local benefit agencies.

Enabling authorized Community Organizations to confirm and validate logged volunteer hours.

Sending system notifications regarding upcoming reporting deadlines, hour verification updates, or account changes.

Improving Platform functionality, security, and user experience.

4. How Information Is Shared
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share your information only in the following contexts:

With Selected Organizations: Beneficiary activity logs and contact details are shared only with the specific Community Partner Organizations linked to the Beneficiary’s account for verification.

With Benefit Agencies (User-Initiated): Activity reports and proofs of compliance are only submitted to government offices (e.g., state SNAP or Medicare administrators) at the explicit direction of the user or linked organization.

Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist in hosting, database management, and communications, bound by strict confidentiality obligations.

Legal Compliance: When required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process.

5. Data Security
We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to safeguard Personal Identifiable Information (PII). Access to account data is restricted to authorized personnel and verified partner organizations requiring access for program administration.

6. User Rights and Data Retention

Access & Correction: Users can review, update, or correct their profile details and logged activities through their account settings.

Data Retention: We retain activity logs as needed to maintain compliance histories for benefit reporting cycles or as required by applicable non-profit record-keeping laws.

Account Deletion: Users may request account deletion by contacting us. Note that deleting an account may impact the ability to retrieve past activity verification logs.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect platform enhancements or legal adjustments. Material updates will be communicated via email or a notification on the Platform prior to becoming effective.
